# Authority Data and Controlled Vocabularies

Lead: This chapter covers authority files (GND, VIAF, Wikidata) and controlled vocabularies (SKOS, thesauri). It argues that linking entities to external authorities is the single highest-leverage move a DH data project can make for reuse and interoperability.

## Learning Goals

- Identify what an authority file is and what a controlled vocabulary is, and how they differ.
- Look up an entity in GND, VIAF, Wikidata and record its persistent identifier.
- Design a small SKOS thesaurus for a domain.
- Recognise the costs and benefits of authority linking in a concrete dataset.
