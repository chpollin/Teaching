# Persistent Identifiers

Lead: A persistent identifier (DOI, Handle, ARK, ORCID, ROR) is a contract that an identifier will continue to resolve. This chapter teaches the PID landscape and the operational practice of minting, registering, and citing PIDs in DH projects.

## Learning Goals

- Distinguish DOI, Handle, ARK, ORCID, ROR, and the URN family by what they identify and who governs them.
- Mint a DOI for a dataset via Zenodo or a comparable repository.
- Embed PIDs in DH artefacts (datasets, editions, code) so they survive author moves.
- Recognise when a URL is enough and when a PID is required.
