# Data Modelling Basics

Lead: This chapter introduces data modelling as a disciplined act of choosing what to represent and how. It covers entity types, relations, attributes, cardinality, and the trade-offs between expressive and tractable models.

## Learning Goals

- Identify entities, relations, and attributes in a small research domain.
- Read and write a simple conceptual model (ER-style or class diagram).
- Distinguish a conceptual model from its serialisation in a specific format.
- Apply the principle "model the question, not the data" to a concrete case.
