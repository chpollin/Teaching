# 00 Foundations

## Overview

The opening block. Sets the vocabulary used by every other block: the three literacies, Computational Thinking as methodological core, and the Promptotyping method as bridge from method to practice. Three chapters, written first as the pilot.

## Learning Goals

After this block, the reader can:

- name the three literacies (Data, Computational, AI) and explain how they intersect.
- list the four Computational Thinking operations and recognise them in a DH task.
- describe the Promptotyping method as a disciplined process for LLM-assisted artefact development.

## Chapters

- `00-01_three-literacies` — Data, Computational, AI Literacy as a connected practice.
- `00-02_computational-thinking` — the four CT operations with DH examples.
- `00-03_promptotyping-overview` — the method as bridge concept.

## Prerequisites

None. This block is the entry point.

## Reading Path

Sequential. The three chapters define each other.
