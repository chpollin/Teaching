# Promptotyping as Method

Lead: This chapter introduces Promptotyping as a disciplined process for LLM-assisted artefact development. It positions the method as the bridge between the foundational concepts (literacies, Computational Thinking) and the applied work the rest of the textbook teaches.

## Learning Goals

- Describe Promptotyping's four phases (Preparation, Exploration, Distillation, Implementation).
- Distinguish Knowledge, Process, and Action documents in a Promptotyping project.
- Recognise where Promptotyping fits in the workflow: not a replacement for thinking, but a structure for the thinking.
- Identify projects where Promptotyping helps and where simpler approaches suffice.
