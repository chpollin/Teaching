# AI Literacy and Ethics

Lead: AI Literacy is the third literacy this textbook integrates. This chapter covers what it means to use AI responsibly in DH: provenance and disclosure, hallucination, model bias, environmental cost, the EU AI Act, and the role of the human as the accountable signatory of research output.

## Learning Goals

- Define AI Literacy along the four dimensions (technical, methodological, ethical, social).
- Disclose AI use in research artefacts (paper, edition, code) with appropriate granularity.
- Reason about hallucination, bias, and environmental cost in operational decisions.
- Apply the EU AI Act framing to a DH workflow and recognise high-risk patterns.
