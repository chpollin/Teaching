# Prompting and Context Engineering

Lead: Prompting is not magic; it is a discipline. This chapter teaches the patterns that move work from brittle one-shot prompts to engineered context: system prompts, role separation, structured output, few-shot examples, and the move from prompt to artefact.

## Learning Goals

- Distinguish zero-shot, few-shot, and chain-of-thought prompting and their costs.
- Engineer context with explicit roles, schemas, and references.
- Constrain output with structured formats (JSON, XML, schemas) and validate it.
- Apply a prompting pattern to a DH task (entity extraction, normalisation, summarisation).
