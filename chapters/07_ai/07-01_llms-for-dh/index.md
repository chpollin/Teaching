# LLMs for DH Developers

Lead: Large Language Models are the substrate of contemporary AI tooling. This chapter introduces transformers, tokenisation, context windows, and the model landscape (Claude, GPT, Gemini, open weights) at a level a DH developer needs to choose, integrate, and trust a model.

## Learning Goals

- Describe the transformer architecture at the level a DH developer needs.
- Distinguish base, instruct, and reasoning models and their trade-offs.
- Read a model card and identify training data, context window, and licence.
- Place an LLM in a DH workflow (transcription, structuring, summarisation, search).
