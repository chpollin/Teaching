# Information Visualisation Fundamentals

Lead: Visualisation is reasoning made visible. This chapter introduces the foundations a DH developer needs: the data-to-visual mapping, Bertin's variables, the task-data-design triangle, and the discipline of choosing a chart that answers a research question rather than decorating one.

## Learning Goals

- Map data types (nominal, ordinal, interval, ratio) to visual variables.
- Read a chart in terms of the task it serves (comparison, distribution, relation, change).
- Distinguish exploratory and explanatory visualisation.
- Apply a simple visualisation grammar to a DH dataset.
