# Visualisation for Editions

Lead: Edition-specific visualisation goes beyond charts: variant graphs, network views of correspondents, geographic itineraries, apparatus dashboards. This chapter closes the textbook by treating the edition itself as a visualisation problem and the reader as the eventual user of the developer's design choices.

## Learning Goals

- Identify the visualisation needs particular to a digital edition.
- Build a correspondents network and a sender-itinerary map from a TEI corpus.
- Design a reader interface that integrates apparatus, register, and image without overwhelming.
- Reason about scholarly trust in a visualisation: legend, source, certainty.
