# Information, Data, Knowledge

Lead: This chapter introduces the DIKW progression (Data, Information, Knowledge, Wisdom) and clarifies what the textbook means when it speaks of data. It frames computing as the disciplined transformation between these levels.

## Learning Goals

- Distinguish data, information, knowledge in operational terms, not philosophical ones.
- Explain why "raw data" is a category mistake and what consequences this has for DH work.
- Identify which level of the DIKW progression a given research task targets.
- Recognise encoding as the act that makes data movable between systems.
