# HTR and Transcription Workflows

Lead: Handwritten Text Recognition (HTR) has moved from research curiosity to working tool. This chapter covers eScriptorium, Transkribus, and Transformer-based recognisers, the production loop from segmentation through training data to PAGE-XML or TEI output, and the operational discipline of CER targets and correction rounds.

## Learning Goals

- Place HTR in the edition pipeline and distinguish it from OCR.
- Run a segmentation and recognition pass with an open tool (eScriptorium, Kraken).
- Read PAGE-XML and convert it to a TEI base layer.
- Reason about CER, training data needs, and the cost-benefit of fine-tuning.
