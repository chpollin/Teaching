# TEI Critical Apparatus

Lead: The critical apparatus is where scholarly judgement becomes data. This chapter teaches the TEI mechanisms for variants, witnesses, and editorial intervention: app/rdg, lem, certainty, and the trade-offs between location-referenced, double-end-point, and parallel-segmentation methods.

## Learning Goals

- Encode a textual variant in TEI with witnesses and certainty.
- Distinguish the three apparatus methods and recognise their trade-offs.
- Model genetic stages with delSpan, addSpan, and the gestures of revision.
- Render an apparatus from TEI for a reader and a machine.
