# Edition Publication

Lead: Publishing an edition is publishing a long-lived research output. This chapter covers the operational layer: from TEI source through XSLT or static-site rendering to deposit (Zenodo, RADAR, institutional repositories), DOIs, and the discipline of versioning a living edition.

## Learning Goals

- Choose between XSLT rendering, static-site generators, and Web Components for a TEI edition.
- Publish an edition with a stable URL, a DOI, and a persistent landing page.
- Version an evolving edition and document the change log.
- Apply FAIR principles to an edition (findable, accessible, interoperable, reusable).
