# Scholarly Editions

Lead: A scholarly edition is more than a transcription. This chapter frames the work of editing as a discipline with critical apparatus, source criticism, and reader scaffolding, and locates the digital edition between the printed tradition and the data-driven future.

## Learning Goals

- Distinguish documentary, critical, and genetic editions.
- Recognise the components of a scholarly edition (text, apparatus, commentary, indices).
- Place a digital edition in the lineage of print and discuss what changes.
- Read a sample edition entry and identify editorial decisions.
