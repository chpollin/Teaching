# Research Data Management

Lead: Research Data Management is the operational discipline that makes FAIR a reality. This chapter covers the data lifecycle, the Data Management Plan as a working document, repositories (Zenodo, RADAR, institutional), and the roles of data steward and project lead in DH.

## Learning Goals

- Map the research data lifecycle to a DH project and identify the open hand-offs.
- Write a Data Management Plan that survives the project, not a compliance artefact.
- Choose a repository (Zenodo, RADAR, institutional) for a DH dataset.
- Apply FAIR and TRUST principles to operational decisions.
