# Project Management for DH

Lead: DH projects sit between research and software. This chapter introduces lightweight management for that hybrid: roles, milestones, lanes, asynchronous coordination, and the discipline of tracking decisions instead of tasks. The aim is enough structure to ship, not bureaucracy.

## Learning Goals

- Define roles and responsibilities for a small DH team (PI, developer, editor, coordinator).
- Plan a project as milestones and lanes rather than dated task lists.
- Run asynchronous coordination with issues, decision logs, and short syncs.
- Distinguish project management from research management and recognise the overlap.
