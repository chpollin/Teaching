# Ontologies: OWL, CIDOC-CRM, RiC-O

Lead: Ontologies are formal models of a domain. This chapter introduces OWL as the logic-based modelling language and walks through three domain ontologies a DH developer encounters: CIDOC-CRM for cultural objects, RiC-O for archives, FOAF/BIO/AgRelOn for people.

## Learning Goals

- Read an OWL ontology and identify classes, properties, and axioms.
- Distinguish OWL profiles (Lite, DL, Full) and recognise which fits a DH use case.
- Choose between CIDOC-CRM, RiC-O, and custom extensions for a domain.
- Recognise the cost of ontology commitment and the discipline of extensions.
