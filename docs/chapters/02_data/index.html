02 Data

Overview

Data Literacy for the DH developer. What a dataset is, how to model it, when to choose XML over CSV, when to introduce a controlled vocabulary, when to commit to a persistent identifier, what FAIR means concretely. Eight chapters, vault-strong.

Learning Goals

After this block, the reader can:

  • read a dataset and identify its structure, types, and metadata.
  • choose between XML, JSON, RDF, CSV for a given task with reasons.
  • design a controlled vocabulary and authority data structure.
  • apply FAIR principles concretely (PIDs, machine-readable metadata, licensing).
  • model humanities data in a chosen format with the right grain.
  • recognise the costs and benefits of normalisation.

Chapters

Eight chapters. Specific slugs assigned during Phase 1.

Prerequisites

Block 01 helps but is not strictly required.

Reading Path

Mostly sequential. Chapters on modelling and chapters on publishing can be taken independently after the first three.
