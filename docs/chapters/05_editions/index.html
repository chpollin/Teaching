05 Editions

Overview

Scholarly editing for the DH developer. TEI markup, critical apparatus, XSLT transformation, IIIF integration, reading interfaces, edition publishing. Substrate: the TTT walkthroughs with Struwwelpeter and Schönberg cases from 2022. Eight chapters.

Learning Goals

After this block, the reader can:

  • model a manuscript or text in TEI with appropriate markup density.
  • encode a critical apparatus (witnesses, variants, sigla, readings).
  • transform TEI to HTML with XSLT.
  • integrate IIIF manifests for facsimile delivery.
  • deploy a TEI edition as a static reading site.
  • understand where LLM assistance helps and where it harms in edition work.

Chapters

Eight chapters. Specific slugs assigned during Phase 1.

Prerequisites

Block 02 for data modelling concepts; block 04 for deployment.

Reading Path

Sequential. Earlier chapters define the vocabulary used in later chapters.
